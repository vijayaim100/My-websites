Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ExpensGlobe handles information. ExpensGlobe is an offline-first, single-page expense tracker.

1. No Online Collection of Expense Data

ExpensGlobe does not require you to create an account and does not send your transaction records to our servers.

The transaction information you enter, including amounts, descriptions, dates, categories, currencies, logs, summaries, and charts, is stored locally on your device or in your browser.

We cannot view, access, or retrieve your expense records from our servers because ExpensGlobe does not upload them to us.

2. Local Storage

ExpensGlobe may use browser storage or device storage to save your settings and expense records. This allows the app to work offline and remember your information on the same device and browser.

Your locally stored data may be removed if you clear your browser data, uninstall the app, reset your device, use private browsing, or manually delete the application data.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure and creating backups of important records. You should not enter passwords, bank login details, complete payment card numbers, or other unnecessary highly sensitive information into ExpensGlobe.

3. Website and Technical Information

The ExpensGlobe application itself does not intentionally collect personal information or track your expense activity.

Our website hosting provider may automatically process limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, device type, and security logs. This information may be used by the hosting provider to deliver and protect the website.

4. Purchases Through Gumroad

If you purchase ExpensGlobe through Gumroad, Gumroad processes the purchase and may provide us with limited customer information, such as your name, email address, purchase information, and license details.

We use this information only when reasonably necessary to confirm your purchase, provide license support, respond to customer questions, send important product-related information, or handle refunds and support requests.

Payment card information is handled by Gumroad and its payment providers. We do not store your complete payment card details.

Gumroad has its own privacy policy and terms that apply to information processed through its platform.

5. Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

We use this information only to reply to you, provide support, troubleshoot problems, and maintain support records when reasonably necessary.

6. Cookies and Tracking

ExpensGlobe does not use advertising cookies or sell personal information.

The website or its hosting provider may use essential technologies needed for security, website delivery, or basic functionality.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored transaction data because we do not receive or store that data on our servers.

Limited purchase or support information may be processed by necessary service providers such as Gumroad, payment processors, email providers, hosting providers, or security providers.

8. Data Deletion

To delete your expense records, use the delete or reset option available in the application, or clear ExpensGlobe's stored data through your browser or device settings.

To request deletion of purchase or support information held by us, contact us at the email address below.

9. Children's Privacy

ExpensGlobe is not intentionally directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application, website, payment process, or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new update date.
